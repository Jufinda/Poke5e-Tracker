    📘 Complete Step-by-Step Poké5e Tabletop Tutorial

    Everything you and your players need to play a full game of Poké5e from start to finish.

    Step 1: Character Creation (Creating Your Trainer & Starter)

    1. Set Trainer Ability Scores: Allocate your Trainer's 6 core stats (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA). Standard array is 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8. Your Level 1 Trainer starts with 10 + CON Modifier HP and 10 + DEX Modifier AC.
    2. Choose a Trainer Path & Specialization: At Level 1, pick a Type Specialization (+1 to skill checks/saves with Pokémon of that type) and at Level 2+ pick your Trainer Path (Ace Trainer, Pokémon Collector, Nurse, Pokéchef, Researcher, Tactician, Commander, or Ranger).
    3. Pick Your Starter Pokémon: Under Pokémon Party, type a starter species with SR ≤ 1/2 (e.g., Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle, Pikachu, Eevee) and click the yellow ⚡ button. It will automatically pull official stats, HP, AC, Ability, and starting moves!
    4. Official Starting Equipment (Core Rules): By Poké5e rules, Level 1 Trainers begin with 5x Poké Balls, 1x Potion, a Pokédex, and ₽1,000.

    Step 2: Exploration, Tall Grass & Spawning Wild Encounters

    1. Skill Checks Outside Combat: When exploring routes, caves, or cities, the GM calls for a d20 + Stat Mod (+ Proficiency Bonus if proficient) roll using the 🎲 Dice Roller.
    2. Spawning a Wild Pokémon: When players enter tall grass, caves, or water, the GM opens 🌿 Wild Encounter & Biomes, selects the current Biome (e.g., Forest), Max SR, and Wild Level, and clicks 🎲 Spawn Wild (leave the species box empty for a random encounter, or pick a specific Pokémon).

    Step 3: How a Full Round of Combat Works (Turn-by-Turn)

    1. Roll Initiative: Every active Pokémon (and Trainer in danger) rolls 1d20 + DEX Modifier. Highest goes first.
    2. On Your Turn, You Have:
      • Movement: Your Pokémon can move up to its listed Speed (e.g., walking 30ft).
      • 1 Action (Choose One):
        • Use a Pokémon Move (1 Action): Click the blue 🎲 button next to one of your Pokémon's 4 moves, then click 🎲 Roll Move (Spend 1 PP).
        • Throw a Poké Ball (Trainer Action): Attempt to catch a weakened wild Pokémon (see Step 4).
        • Use a Medicine/Item (Trainer Action): Click Use next to a Potion or Berry in your Interactive Inventory to heal a Pokémon.
        • Switch Pokémon / Dodge / Dash / Help: Swap your active Pokémon or take standard 5e defensive actions.
      • 1 Bonus Action: Used for quick moves (like Quick Attack or Agility) or Trainer Path commands.
      • 1 Reaction (Per Round): Used for Opportunity Attacks when an enemy leaves melee range or moves like Protect.
    3. Resolving Move Attacks & Damage:
      • Attack Roll Moves: The tracker rolls 1d20 + Move Stat Mod + PB + Bond. If the total meets or beats the target's AC, it hits!
      • Saving Throw Moves: The target rolls 1d20 + Stat Mod against the move's Save DC (8 + Move Stat Mod + PB).
      • Type Effectiveness: Check the target Pokémon's top Weak / Resist / Immune banner. Multiply final damage by 2x (Super Effective) or ½x (Not Very Effective). STAB (Same-Type Attack Bonus) is automatically added by the roller!
    4. End of Turn Status Checks: Burned or Poisoned Pokémon take chip damage equal to their Proficiency Bonus at the end of their turn. Asleep/Frozen/Confused Pokémon roll to recover.

    Step 4: Catching Wild Pokémon

    1. Weaken the Wild Pokémon: Reduce its Wild HP and/or inflict a Status Condition (Asleep, Paralyzed, Burned, etc.) in the Wild Encounter panel. Watch the green Capture DC drop in real time!
    2. Select Your Poké Ball: In the Wild Encounter panel, choose which ball you are throwing (e.g., Poké Ball (+0), Great Ball (+5), Ultra Ball (+10)) and select the throwing Trainer.
    3. Roll Animal Handling / Throw Check: Roll 1d20 + Animal Handling (or Poké Ball throw bonus). If you meet or beat the Capture DC, click + Catch!
    4. Automatic Inventory Rule Check: Clicking + Catch checks if that Trainer actually has the selected Poké Ball in their Interactive Inventory. If they do, 1 ball is deducted and the Pokémon is added to their party! If not, it alerts the Combat Log.

    Step 5: Resting, Leveling Up & Evolution

    1. Short Rest (1 Hour): Click the green ⛺ Hit Die button on any Pokémon card to roll its Hit Die + CON modifier and recover HP between battles.
    2. Long Rest / Pokécenter (8 Hours): Click 🏥 Long Rest on a Trainer or 🏥 Pokécenter Party Rest in the top bar to restore 100% HP, all Move PP, and cure all Status Conditions.
    3. Leveling & Evolution: When Pokémon gain levels, increase their Lvl box (auto-scales HP & proficiency). When a Pokémon reaches its evolution requirement, an ✨ Evolve button automatically appears on its card!

    📜 Poké5e Core Rules, Status Conditions, Natures & Bonds Reference

    Quick table reference for all core rules, combat formulas, and appendix mechanics

    ⚔️ Core Combat & Tabletop Formulas

    Move Attack & Save DC

    • Attack Roll: d20 + Move Power Mod + PB + Bond
    • Move Save DC: 8 + Move Power Mod + PB
    • STAB Bonus: Adds Proficiency Bonus (PB) to damage when move type matches Pokémon's type.

    Pokéslots & Max Control SR

    • Max SR by Trainer Lvl: Lvl 1-2 (SR 2), Lvl 3-5 (SR 5), Lvl 6-7 (SR 6), Lvl 8-10 (SR 8), Lvl 11-13 (SR 10), Lvl 14-16 (SR 12), Lvl 17+ (SR 15+).
    • Pokéslots: 3 slots (Lvl 1), 4 slots (Lvl 5), 5 slots (Lvl 10), 6 slots (Lvl 15). Collector Path adds +1 slot.

    Capture DC Formula

    • Formula: 10 + Floor(SR) + Floor(Lvl/2) + Floor(HP% × 10) - Status(5) - BallBonus
    • Ball Bonuses: Poké Ball (+0), Great Ball (+5), Ultra Ball (+10), Specialty Balls (+15), Master Ball (Auto).

    ❤️ Loyalty & Bond Levels (-3 to +3) & Transformations

    Bond Level Modifiers

    • Bond +3 (Loyal): +3 to Move Attack Rolls & Saving Throws, +1d6 Loyalty inspiration.
    • Bond +1 / +2: +1 or +2 to Attack Rolls.
    • Bond 0: Neutral.
    • Bond -1 to -3 (Disloyal): Penalty to attack rolls; may disobey commands in combat.

    Transformations (Mega, Dynamax, Z-Move, Tera)

    • Mega Evolution: +2 AC and double Move Stat Modifier on damage rolls.
    • Dynamax (3 Rounds): Double current/max HP and roll damage dice twice (take higher).
    • Z-Move (Once/Rest): Move automatically hits or increases Save DC by +5, plus double damage dice.
    • Terastallize: Changes defensive type to Tera Type; grants double STAB if matching original type.
