    📘 Poké5e Campaign Hub — Step-by-Step Guide

    Everything you need to run a smooth multiplayer tabletop campaign session.

    👑 Step 1: Hosting or Joining a Room

    When you open the tracker, type your name into the Your Name / Handle box. If you are the Dungeon Master / Game Master, click 👑 Host Game. This creates a secret 4-letter room code (e.g. X7K2) and assigns you full GM editing permissions. Players can open the same link on their phones or browsers, type their name, enter the 4-letter code, and click Join Room to sync into the party in real-time!

    👥 Step 2: Creating Trainers & Pokémon

    As the Game Master, click + Add Trainer to create a character sheet. You can set their Level, Trainer Path (e.g., Ace Trainer, Nurse, Collector), and Type Specialization. Each trainer automatically calculates their Max Pokéslots and Maximum Controllable Species Rating (SR). Click + Add Pokémon Slot to add their partner Pokémon.

    ⚡ Step 3: Forked Auto-Fill & Inventory

    In the Pokémon species input, type a name (like Pikachu or Charizard) or use the autocomplete dropdown. Click the yellow ⚡ button to instantly fetch that species' official base stats, elemental types, ability, and starting moves directly from your GitHub fork! For items, use the inventory item picker to select items like Poké Ball or Potion, specify a quantity, and click + Add.

    🎲 Step 4: Rolling Moves & Combat

    Once a Pokémon has moves loaded, clicking the blue 🎲 button next to any move opens its full rule inspector and lets you roll its attack, save DC, and level-scaled damage dice directly into the shared session log—automatically factoring in STAB (Same-Type Attack Bonus), Natures (+2/-2), Bonds, and active global Weather & Terrain.

    🏕️ Step 5: Resting & Wild Encounters

    Use the 🏥 Pokécenter Party Rest button at the top to instantly restore 100% HP, Move PP, and clear Status Conditions for everyone after a long rest, or use ⛺ Hit Dice on individual Pokémon cards for short rests. Use the 🌿 Wild Encounter & Biomes panel to filter wild Pokémon by Biome and SR, roll Shinys (1%), and calculate live Pokéball Capture DCs!

    📜 Poké5e Core Rules, Status Conditions, Natures & Bonds Reference

    Quick table reference for all core rules and appendix mechanics

    ⚡ Core Rules & Mechanics (How to Play)

    1. Ability Checks & Skills

    Roll a d20 + Ability Modifier + Proficiency Bonus (if proficient). Used for Acrobatics, Athletics, Perception, Stealth, Animal Handling, and out-of-combat exploration.

    2. Saving Throws

    Roll a d20 + Ability Modifier + Proficiency (if proficient) against a Hazard or Move Save DC (8 + PB + Move Power Mod). Used to resist status conditions and area damage.

    3. Combat & Initiative

    Roll Initiative (d20 + DEX mod). On your turn, you get 1 Action (Move, Attack, Cast Move, Use Item, Dash), 1 Bonus Action, and movement equal to your Speed.

    4. Catching Pokémon (DC Formula)

    Capture DC = 10 + SR + (Level ÷ 2) + (Current HP %) - Status Bonus - Ball Bonus. Lower their HP and inflict status (Sleep/Paralysis) to make catching easier!

    ⚡ Status Conditions (Appendix)

    ❤️ Loyalty & Bonds (-3 to +3)

    • +3 Bond: +2 to Saving Throws, +1 to Attack Rolls, immune to charm/frighten while Trainer is conscious.
    • +2 Bond: +1 to Saving Throws and +1 to Attack Rolls.
    • +1 Bond: +1 to Saving Throws.
    • 0 Bond: Neutral loyalty (No bonus or penalty).
    • -1 Bond: Disadvantage on Loyalty/Obedience checks.
    • -2 Bond: -1 to Attack Rolls; may ignore commands on failed CHA check.
    • -3 Bond: Disobeys commands in combat unless Trainer succeeds DC 15 Animal Handling.

    🏕️ Resting, Fainting & Transformation Rules

    • Short Rest (1 hr): Spend Hit Dice (up to Level) + CON mod to heal HP.
    • Long Rest (8 hrs) / Pokécenter: Restores 100% HP, 100% Move PP, and cures non-permanent Status Conditions.
    • STAB (Same-Type Attack Bonus): Adds Proficiency Bonus (+2 to +6) to damage when Move Type matches Pokémon Type.
    • Mega / Z-Move / Dynamax / Terastal: Once per Long Rest transformation; Tera grants STAB on chosen Tera Type.
