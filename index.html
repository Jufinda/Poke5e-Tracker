    📘 Complete Step-by-Step Poké5e Tabletop Tutorial

    Everything you and your players need to play a full game of Poké5e from start to finish.

    1. Automated Trainer vs. Trainer & Wild Battles

    Click the 🎲 button next to any move on a Pokémon card (or on a Wild Pokémon). Pick the Target Pokémon from the dropdown and click ⚔️ Execute Move. The tracker automatically checks Attack Roll vs. AC (or rolls the Target's Saving Throw against your Move DC), calculates Weaknesses/Resistances, deducts 1 PP, and subtracts the HP from the target!

    2. Combat Buffs, Debuffs (Growl, Smokescreen) & Disadvantage

    Every Pokémon card has a ⚔️ Combat Modifiers & Debuffs bar where you can set Normal / Advantage / Disadvantage, Atk Mod, Dmg Mod (stackable up to -5 for Growl), and a Smokescreen (-1d4) checkbox. These automatically apply whenever that Pokémon rolls a move!

    3. Tactical Battle Map & Movement Speeds

    Click 🗺️ Room & Battle Map in the header to open the 5ft-square grid. Select any Trainer or Pokémon token and click a square to move them—it tracks how many feet they've moved this turn against their walking/flying speed and measures exact distances between tokens.

    📜 Poké5e Core Rules, Medicine Formulas & Status Conditions

    Quick table reference for all core rules, healing dice, and combat formulas

    Move Attack & Save DC

    • Attack Roll: d20 + Move Power Mod + PB + Bond
    • Move Save DC: 8 + Move Power Mod + PB
    • STAB Bonus: Adds Proficiency Bonus (PB) to damage when move type matches Pokémon's type.

    Official Medicine Healing

    • Potion: 2d4 + 2 HP
    • Super Potion: 4d4 + 4 HP
    • Hyper Potion: 8d4 + 8 HP
    • Max Potion / Full Restore: Full HP (+ cures status)

    Capture DC Formula

    • Formula: 10 + Floor(SR) + Floor(Lvl/2) + Floor(HP% × 10) - Status(5) - BallBonus
